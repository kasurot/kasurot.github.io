How I Can Help

Most of my client work is delivered through GCA Technology Services, where I lead the professional services practice. If you are standing up an identity program, absorbing an acquisition, or holding an audit finding you cannot evidence, these are the problems I work on.

Where I'm Useful

Identity Program Leadership
  • Roadmap, operating model, and governance for an identity function that has grown by accretion.
  • Vendor-neutral architecture across Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, including CIAM.
  • Standing privileged access reduction, with the control evidence to show it held.
Non-Human Identity Governance
  • Inventory the service principals, app registrations, managed identities, and credentials that hold real permissions.
  • An ownership register reconciled to the directory, with a named person who can get the wrong ones deleted.
  • Reduce standing access for workloads without breaking the integrations that depend on it.
Post-Acquisition Identity Risk Review
  • Rapid assessment of identity sprawl, privileged access exposure, and integration risk across merged environments.
  • Remediation sequenced by blast radius rather than acquisition date.
  • Written findings a board or investment committee can act on.
Compliance Evidence & Audit Readiness
  • Access certification, separation of duties, and entitlement governance that produce evidence rather than activity.
  • SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIST-aligned control mapping, including SOX-scoped IT general controls for public companies.
  • Controls designed to survive a sample, not just a screenshot.
Executive Briefings & Workshops
  • Board and steering-committee sessions on identity risk, non-human identity, and the governance of AI agents.
  • Architecture reviews and roadmap working sessions with the teams that will have to run the result.

Who This Fits

  • Mid-market and enterprise organizations where identity has become the constraint on an acquisition, an audit, or a cloud migration.
  • Private equity and portfolio companies that need the identity risk in a target surfaced before close, and a plan to remediate after it.
  • Teams that inherited an estate and need to know what they own before they can decide what to keep.
