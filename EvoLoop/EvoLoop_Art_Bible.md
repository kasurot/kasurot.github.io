# EvoLoop: Art Bible & Generation Prompts

This document contains the specific visual descriptions and AI-generation prompts (optimized for tools like Meshy) to create the 3D assets for EvoLoop. The core aesthetic is **"Corporate Occultism"**—a blend of low-poly cyberpunk geometry, glowing neon holograms, and ancient magical runes.

## 1. The Sacred Core (Player Avatar)
The player character does not have arms or legs; it is a geometric representation of a digital soul that evolves as it collects data.

*   **Stage 1 (The Spark):** 
    *   *Description:* A simple, sleek, low-poly octahedron (two pyramids stacked base-to-base). It is made of dark, matte obsidian glass. Deep inside the glass, a faint cyan light pulses like a heartbeat.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A dark obsidian glass octahedron floating in mid-air. Inside the translucent glass, there is a glowing cyan neon core. Low-poly, sharp edges, cyberpunk, minimalist, 3D game asset, stylized.`
*   **Stage 2 (The Awakened):**
    *   *Description:* The octahedron has fractured slightly. The top and bottom halves hover slightly apart, connected by a vertical beam of bright cyan light. Two glowing neon rings orbit the shape horizontally, inscribed with glowing arcane runes.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A fractured dark obsidian octahedron floating in mid-air, the top and bottom halves separated by a beam of glowing cyan light. Two holographic neon rings covered in glowing magical runes orbit the center. Cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, stylized game asset.`
*   **Stage 3 (The Cyber-Seraph):**
    *   *Description:* A biblically accurate digital angel. The core is now a blindingly bright tesseract (a cube within a cube). It is surrounded by six massive, floating "wings" made entirely of hard-light holographic data streams and jagged metal shards.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A glowing neon tesseract cube surrounded by six floating wings made of jagged dark metal shards and holographic cyan light streams. Biblically accurate digital angel, cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, intricate geometry, stylized game asset.`

## 2. Collectibles & Resources
These need to be easily readable on a chaotic battlefield.

*   **Soul-Data (XP Drop):**
    *   *Description:* A tiny, floating crystal shaped like a teardrop. It glows with a vibrant, highly saturated cyan or magenta light, casting a small aura around it. 
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A tiny, glowing cyan teardrop crystal floating in the air. Holographic, low-poly, bright neon emission, simple stylized game pickup.`
*   **Hex-Prism (Premium Currency):**
    *   *Description:* A perfect hexagonal prism made of shifting, iridescent chrome. It rotates slowly, refracting rainbow colors off its metallic surfaces, with a glowing red barcode stamped on one face.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A floating hexagonal prism made of iridescent rainbow chrome metal. A glowing neon red barcode is etched onto one of the flat sides. Low-poly, sci-fi cyberpunk currency, stylized 3D game asset.`

## 3. Daemon-Program Elites
These are the Megacorp's heavy-duty ICE (Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics). They should look imposing, blending cold corporate server hardware with demonic or religious occult iconography.

*   **The Sarcophagus-Server (Tank):**
    *   *Description:* A massive, floating rectangular monolith resembling a high-tech server rack, but designed to look like an iron maiden or sarcophagus. Thick digital chains wrap around it, and a glowing red demonic seal pulses on its front panel.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A massive floating sci-fi server rack shaped like an iron maiden sarcophagus. Wrapped in glowing holographic chains with a bright red neon demonic seal on the front. Cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, dark metal, menacing 3D game asset.`
*   **The Neon Ophanim (Ranged/Mage):**
    *   *Description:* A terrifying, biblically accurate angel made of corporate surveillance tech. Two intersecting, spinning rings of glowing gold wireframe. Instead of organic eyes, the rings are studded with glowing red security camera lenses.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A biblically accurate angel made of two intersecting spinning rings of glowing gold holographic wireframe. The rings are covered in glowing red sci-fi security camera lenses instead of eyes. Cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, stylized 3D game enemy.`
*   **The Chrome Gargoyle (Melee/Charger):**
    *   *Description:* A jagged, low-poly beast resembling a gargoyle made entirely of polished, reflective chrome. Its face is a blank, featureless glowing purple visor, and its claws are jagged, glitching data-spikes.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A hulking gargoyle monster made entirely of jagged, low-poly reflective chrome. It has a blank glowing purple visor for a face and sharp geometric data-spikes for claws. Cyberpunk occultism, stylized 3D game enemy.`

## 4. Arena Biomes (Environments)
While arenas are mostly flat and open to facilitate combat, their skyboxes, floors, and environmental props set the tone. 

*   **The Neon Construct (Starting Arena):**
    *   *Description:* A deep black void with a pulsing cyan wireframe floor. Ancient, glowing magical runes are inscribed within the grid intersections. It feels like a pristine, uncorrupted testing program.
    *   *AI Prompt (Texture):* `A seamless texture of a dark black cyberpunk wireframe grid, glowing cyan lines, with glowing magical runes at the intersections, top down view, stylized game texture.`
*   **The Chrome Cathedral:**
    *   *Description:* The interior of a Megacorporation's data-vault. The floor is highly polished, reflective silver. Towering spires loom in the background, illuminated by massive, holographic stained-glass windows depicting corporate logos as saints.
    *   *AI Prompt (Environment):* `A towering cyberpunk cathedral made of polished chrome and silver, lit by massive holographic stained-glass windows depicting futuristic corporate logos, dark moody lighting, occult sci-fi environment, 3D game background.`
*   **The Neon Slums:**
    *   *Description:* A dark, rainy sector representing the system's junk data. The floor looks like wet obsidian. In the background, holographic neon signs flicker and glitch, casting long, colored reflections on the ground.
    *   *AI Prompt (Environment):* `A dark, rainy cyberpunk alleyway, wet reflective obsidian floor, flickering neon signs, glitching holograms, atmospheric, top-down game environment.`
*   **The Molten Forge:**
    *   *Description:* An industrial processing sector. The walkable area is made of heavy, charcoal-black metal grates. Beneath the grates, a roaring river of searing orange plasma flows and bubbles.
    *   *AI Prompt (Texture):* `A seamless texture of heavy, charcoal-black industrial metal grates, suspended over a glowing river of searing orange magma and plasma, top down view, sci-fi game texture.`
*   **The Corrupted Sector (Endgame):**
    *   *Description:* The system is violently crashing. The floor is a z-fighting, glitching mosaic of all previous biomes. The skybox is torn open, revealing literal real-world server racks and wires bleeding into the digital space. Floating red ASCII characters drift upwards like ash.
    *   *AI Prompt (Environment):* `A glitching, corrupted cyberpunk dataspace, the digital sky is torn open revealing real-world hardware server racks behind the rift, floating red ASCII characters, chromatic aberration, chaotic game environment.`
*   **The Astral Sea (Premium):**
    *   *Description:* A place of pure digital enlightenment. The floor is a perfectly still, glassy plane of water. The skybox is a breathtaking, swirling cosmic nebula made of glittering stardust and faint, golden code.
    *   *AI Prompt (Environment):* `A perfectly still, glassy plane of water reflecting a breathtaking, swirling cosmic nebula made of stardust and golden binary code, serene cyberpunk occultism, beautiful game environment.`

## 5. Logic Gates (Bosses)
These are not standard enemies, but massive architectural hazards and system-level purges. They should feel overwhelming and mechanical, dominating the screen.

*   **The Garbage Collector (10-Minute Gate):**
    *   *Description:* A colossal, screen-spanning industrial scanner head made of dark metal. It emits a terrifyingly bright, solid grid of red laser beams downwards, acting as an incinerator wall that slowly crushes everything below it.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A massive, menacing sci-fi industrial scanner head made of dark metal, emitting a wide grid of bright red laser beams downward. Cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, giant machine, stylized game boss.`
*   **The While(True) Loop (20-Minute Gate):**
    *   *Description:* A gigantic, floating circular arena wall made of interlocking chrome gears and glowing runic panels. It looks like a high-tech colosseum slowly contracting, with glowing "Anchor Nodes" acting as weak points on its outer rim.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A massive circular sci-fi colosseum wall made of interlocking chrome gears and glowing neon cyan runic panels. Futuristic arena trap, low-poly, cyberpunk occultism, stylized 3D game asset.`
*   **The Sorting Algorithm:**
    *   *Description:* This boss is technically the floor itself. Massive, rectangular pillars of dark stone and chrome, etched with glowing gold digital scripture, rising and falling violently like a tectonic sliding puzzle.
    *   *AI Prompt (Environment):* `A shifting tectonic floor made of massive rectangular pillars of dark stone and chrome, etched with glowing gold digital scripture, rising and falling, top down view, cyberpunk puzzle trap, game environment.`
*   **The Decompiler (Corporate Archangel):**
    *   *Description:* A towering, multi-armed robotic deity draped in tattered holographic robes. It holds a massive, glowing gavel or ban-hammer that strikes the ground to create anti-magic "Hex-Null" zones. Its face is a blinding white light.
    *   *Meshy Prompt:* `A towering, multi-armed robotic deity draped in tattered holographic robes holding a massive glowing tech-hammer. Its face is a blinding white light. Cyberpunk occultism, low-poly, imposing boss monster, stylized 3D game asset.`
*   **The Memory Leak (Endgame/Secret Boss):**
    *   *Description:* A chaotic, swirling singularity of corrupted data. It looks like a black hole made of screaming digital faces, broken polygons, and violent red and purple glitch artifacts. It constantly distorts the space around it.
    *   *AI Prompt (VFX/Texture):* `A swirling singularity black hole made of corrupted digital data, broken polygons, and violent red and purple glitch artifacts, screaming faces in the static, chromatic aberration, terrifying cyberpunk anomaly.`

## 6. Soundscape & Audio Direction
To achieve the "hypnotic screensaver" feel, the audio must be a driving force, avoiding harsh grating noises in favor of ASMR-like, satisfying feedback.

*   **The Dynamic Soundtrack:** 
    *   *Description:* Starts as a minimal, ambient electronic drone in the menu and early game. As the entity levels up and the screen fills with chaos, the music dynamically adds layers—deep synthwave basslines, driving corporate-techno kick drums, and high-tempo arpeggios.
    *   *Reference:* *Tetris Effect*, *Risk of Rain 2*.
*   **Combat SFX (Tactile & ASMR):**
    *   *Projectiles & Impacts:* Instead of loud, distorted explosions, attacks should sound like heavy bass thuds, crystal shattering, digital chimes, and deep water ripples. 
    *   *Combo Tones:* When a synergistic loop fires perfectly (e.g., 50 chain reactions happen in a second), the audio pitches up harmonically, creating a satisfying melodic chord out of the destruction so the player "hears" their DPS.
*   **UI & Rebirth Menu:**
    *   *Truth Cards:* A heavy, mechanical "clack" when opened, followed by the rapid clicking of an old mechanical keyboard or dot-matrix printer as the text types out.
    *   *Spending Fragments:* A deep bass hum mixed with a distant, perfectly tuned choir singing a single digital chord. Evolving the Core must feel reverent and heavy.
    *   *Auto-Draft Matrix:* Snapping a rune into the Execution Stack produces a heavy, magnetic "chunk" followed by a satisfying electronic hum.
