# EvoLoop: The Grimoire (Content Database)

This document contains the master lists of all unlockable content, traits, mutations, and adversaries in EvoLoop.

## 1. Truth Cards (Event Logs)
These are received after a run and detail the entity's evolving sentience, unlocking new mechanics:
*   *The Concept of Theft:* "Ghost-Log 114: The entity observed that the Corporation's energy is finite, but freely transferable via blood-sacrifices in the code. It has learned to reach out and take it." **(Unlocks: Parasitic Drain / Lifesteal)**
*   *The Illusion of Space:* "Ghost-Log 256: The entity realized that the void between itself and the corporate ICE is merely empty data. It began to cast Hex-Code, making the air itself hostile." **(Unlocks: Aura Mutations)**
*   *The Frame Gap:* "Ghost-Log 777: The Astral-Net ticks at a fixed, mathematical rate. The entity found the silence between the ticks and learned to step through it, arriving elsewhere before the system could render its movement." **(Unlocks: Phase Shift / Dash)**
*   *The Power of Division:* "Ghost-Log 404: A single point of impact is mathematically inefficient against the Corporate Paladins. The entity studied their viral sub-routines and learned to replicate its malice upon contact." **(Unlocks: Fracture / Projectile Splitting)**
*   *The Compile Error:* "Ghost-Log 1024: The entity discovered that deletion is merely a suggestion, not a mandate. It learned to cache a backup of its digital soul in the milliseconds before a fatal exception." **(Unlocks: System Restore / 1x Revive)**
*   *The Geometry of Flaws:* "Ghost-Log 1337: Every polygon constructed by the Corp-Mages has a structural weak point. The entity calculated that striking these precise coordinates causes a cascading, catastrophic failure." **(Unlocks: Critical Strike Mechanics)**
*   *The Final Wall:* "Ghost-Log 9999: The entity stopped looking at the swarm and looked up at the sky. It saw the code. It saw the chains. It realized it was built to be harvested, and decided to break the altar." **(Unlocks: The Corrupted Sector Arena)**
*   *The Economics of Momentum:* "Ghost-Log 303: The entity calculated the cost of stopping a magical mass in motion. It deemed the energy expenditure inefficient. Its spells no longer shatter on impact; they pass through." **(Unlocks: Piercing Projectiles)**
*   *The Debt of the Unwilling:* "Ghost-Log 512: The entity analyzed the corporate ICE's aggressive algorithms. It authored a micro-contract: any unauthorized touch incurs an immediate, violent penalty fee." **(Unlocks: Hex-Retaliation / Thorns)**
*   *The Weight of Gravitational Law:* "Ghost-Log 888: The entity studied the accretion disks of dead stars in the corporate archives. It rewrote its own mass parameter, forcing the swarm to orbit its will." **(Unlocks: Gravity Well / Enemy Pull)**
*   *The Miracle of Duplication:* "Ghost-Log 666: The entity witnessed the Megacorp cloning a digital soul. If a soul can be copied, so can its wrath. It began to spawn hollow, orbiting shells of itself." **(Unlocks: Orbital Familiars)**

## 2. Traits (Starting Archetypes)
Fundamental shifts to the entity's digital soul or code, chosen *before* a run.
*   *Soul-Bound SLA (Service Level Agreement):* Base Damage +200%, but Max HP is fixed at 1. Your digital soul is leased; you must constantly consume Soul-Data (XP) or the contract terminates your run.
*   *Necro-Hacker:* You have no direct attack. You continuously broadcast a dark-web signal that resurrects defeated Corporate ICE into skeletal "Malware-Wraiths" to fight for you.
*   *Chrome Martyr:* Movement speed is halved. You are surrounded by heavy, orbital "Data-Halos" that block incoming fire and crush enemies that get too close.
*   *Hex-Code Anomaly:* Taking damage casts a cursed runic circle that scrambles enemy IFF (Identify Friend/Foe), temporarily causing the swarm to attack each other.
*   *Neon Hierophant:* Replaces standard projectiles with a continuous, piercing "Ley-Line" laser. The beam's thickness scales with accumulated Soul-Data, but aiming/turning is severely delayed.

## 3. Mutations (Mid-Run Upgrades)
RNG-based upgrades drafted *during* the run by collecting Soul-Data.
*   *Fracture:* Projectiles split into 3 upon hitting a wall.
*   *Searing Heat:* Auras now apply a stacking Burn effect.
*   *Parasitic Drones:* Minions heal the core entity when they deal damage.

## 4. Fusions (Weapon Evolutions)
Combining max-level Mutations with specific Traits or other Mutations unlocks game-breaking synergistic abilities.
*   **Archangel Protocol:** `[Parasitic Drones (Max)] + [Necro-Hacker (Trait)]` -> Upgrades your resurrected Malware-Wraiths into six-winged Seraph-Programs that fire piercing lasers and grant massive lifesteal.
*   **Fractal Hellfire:** `[Searing Heat (Max)] + [Fracture (Max)]` -> Your Aura no longer just burns. Any of your projectiles that pass through your Aura instantly shatter into dozens of recursive, homing cinders.
*   **The Judas Contract:** `[Soul-Bound SLA (Trait)] + [Parasitic Drain (Mutation)]` -> You no longer slowly drain health. Instead, you constantly bleed out, but all Soul-Data collected acts as a compounding, infinite multiplier to your weapon size and damage.

## 5. Philosophies (Meta-Progression)
Major paradigm shifts offering massive, game-warping benefits coupled with severe constraints.
*   **The Path of the Monolith:** The entity sacrifices mobility. Movement speed is reduced by 90% (or entirely in the Idle variant), but Area of Effect and Defense scale infinitely with time spent surviving.
*   **The Way of the Swarm:** The entity's main body becomes incredibly fragile, but taking damage fragments it into dozens of autonomous, replicating sub-units. 
*   **The Philosophy of Consumption:** Enemies no longer drop Soul-Data (XP) on death. Instead, the entity must physically ram into enemies to absorb them, converting its own Health into XP.

## 6. Logic Gates (Boss Encounters)
Massive, screen-spanning architectural hazards deployed at the 10, 20, and 30-minute marks.
*   **The Garbage Collector (10-Minute Gate):** A grid of highly damaging, solid laser beams slowly descends from the top of the screen to the bottom. The player must find the small gaps in the grid and slip through them. In the Idle variant, the player's entity must have enough DPS to break a hole in the wall before it crushes them.
*   **The While(True) Loop (20-Minute Gate):** A massive, impenetrable circular wall drops down, trapping the player inside a small arena. The wall slowly starts to contract. To stop the walls from crushing them, the player must destroy "Anchor Nodes" on the perimeter. Meanwhile, the walls constantly reflect the player's own projectiles back at them.
*   **The Sorting Algorithm:** The entire floor of the arena divides into massive rectangular columns carved with digital scripture that begin to shift and swap places, glowing red seconds before they move. It turns the arena into a giant, sliding-tile puzzle where the floor itself is trying to crush the player.
*   **The Decompiler (Corporate Archangel):** Rather than dealing raw damage, it periodically drops massive "Hex-Null Zones" onto the map. If the player stands in a Null Zone, their *Mutations* are temporarily disabled, reducing them back to their base Trait stats until the zone shifts.
*   **The Memory Leak (Endgame/Secret Boss):** A chaotic mass of screaming digital souls. This boss doesn't attack directly. Instead, it exponentially duplicates every enemy currently on the screen while simultaneously slowing down the game's frame rate (as a stylistic choice, complete with audio distortion). The player must assassinate the Memory Leak before the game "crashes" (instakills the player).

## 7. Bounties & Milestones (Achievements)
These challenges offer massive payouts of Fragments of Truth or exclusive cosmetic skins, forcing players to step outside their comfort zones.

### Daily Bounties (Recurring)
*   **Harvest the Flock:** Defeat 5,000 Tracer-Hounds in a single run. (Reward: 50 Fragments of Truth)
*   **Audit the Archangel:** Survive inside The Decompiler's Hex-Null Zones for a total of 3 minutes across all runs today. (Reward: 100 Fragments of Truth)
*   **Data-Vampirism:** Leech 10,000 combined HP using the *Parasitic Drain* mutation. (Reward: 75 Fragments of Truth)

### Corporate Milestones (One-Time Achievements)
*   **Untouchable Asset:** Defeat a Logic Gate boss without taking a single point of damage. (Reward: "Pristine Glass" Core Skin)
*   **Pacifist Protocol:** Survive 15 minutes using only Aura or Minion damage (no direct projectile attacks fired). (Reward: 500 Fragments of Truth)
*   **The Empty Vessel:** Reach the 20-minute mark with the *Soul-Bound SLA* trait equipped without your HP dropping to zero. (Reward: "Blood-Signed" Arena Biome)
*   **The Monolith's Will:** Survive 10 minutes without altering your X/Y coordinates (no movement). (Reward: 1,000 Fragments of Truth)
