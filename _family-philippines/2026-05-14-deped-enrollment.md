The Department of Education (DepEd) announced a record enrollment of over **30 million students** for School Year 2026–2027, marking the highest enrollment figure in the country's history.

**Breakdown by level:**
- **Elementary** — 15.2 million
- **Junior High School** — 9.1 million
- **Senior High School** — 5.7 million (+8% vs SY 2025–2026)

The biggest increase was seen in Senior High School completers transitioning to higher education institutions or Technical-Vocational and Livelihood (TVL) tracks, reflecting growing interest in skills-based education pathways.

DepEd Secretary Sara Duterte credited the rise to continued improvements in school accessibility, the expansion of the Alternative Learning System (ALS), and the full normalization of in-person classes nationwide.

However, teacher groups reiterated calls for additional classroom construction and more teaching personnel to address the persistent student-to-teacher ratio gap, particularly in rural areas.
