title: "Oil Firms Slash Pump Prices by Over P2 per Liter — Biggest Rollback in Months"
date: 2026-05-17
description: "Major oil companies cut gasoline by ₱2.30 and diesel by ₱1.80 per liter, driven by declining global crude prices and a strengthening peso."
---

Major oil companies in the Philippines announced a significant price rollback effective Tuesday, bringing relief to motorists and transport groups.

**The cuts:**
- **Gasoline** — down ₱2.30 per liter
- **Diesel** — down ₱1.80 per liter
- **Kerosene** — down ₱1.60 per liter

Industry analysts attributed the rollback to declining global crude oil prices amid concerns over slowing demand in major economies, coupled with a strengthening Philippine peso against the US dollar.

Transport groups welcomed the move, saying it could help stabilize fares, but also noted that prices remain elevated compared to pre-pandemic levels.

The Department of Energy said it will continue to monitor pump prices and urged oil firms to be transparent about their pricing formulas.
