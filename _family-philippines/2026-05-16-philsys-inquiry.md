description: "The Senate investigates thousands of transaction failures and identity mismatches in the Philippine Identification System, raising data privacy questions."
---

The Senate committee on public information launched a formal investigation on Monday into the integration and data privacy issues plaguing the Philippine Identification System (PhilSys or national ID system).

Thousands of Filipinos have reported transaction failures, identity mismatches, and delays in receiving their physical IDs since the system's latest rollout expanded to government service portals.

**Key concerns raised:**
- Integration failures with PhilHealth, SSS, and the Bureau of Internal Revenue
- Reports of mismatched biometric data causing transaction rejections
- Questions about the security of personally identifiable information stored in the central database

Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) officials assured the committee that they are working on a system upgrade, expected to be completed within 60 days, to address the integration issues and enhance data protection measures.

Senator Grace Poe, who chaired the hearing, emphasized the need for accountability, stating that a flawed national ID system undermines public trust in digital government services.
