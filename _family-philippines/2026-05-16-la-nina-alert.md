title: "Pagasa Raises La Niña Alert — Heavy Rains Batter Visayas and Mindanao"
date: 2026-05-16

---

State weather bureau PAGASA officially raised a **La Niña Alert** on Friday, warning that the climate pattern is expected to bring above-average rainfall beginning in June.

Parts of Eastern Visayas and Mindanao have already experienced widespread flooding over the past 48 hours, with several municipalities in Leyte and Surigao del Norte reporting waist-deep floodwaters.

**PAGASA advisory highlights:**
- La Niña conditions expected to strengthen through June–August
- Above-normal rainfall likely, especially along the eastern seaboard
- Potential for enhanced southwest monsoon (habagat) activity
- Local governments advised to prepare for landslides and flash floods

The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC) has been placed on full alert, with relief supplies prepositioned in vulnerable areas. Residents in low-lying and mountainous areas are urged to stay updated on local weather bulletins and evacuation protocols.
