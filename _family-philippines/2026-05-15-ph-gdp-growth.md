The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that the country's Gross Domestic Product (GDP) expanded by 6.3% in the first quarter of 2026, surpassing market expectations of 5.9%.

**Key drivers of growth:**
- **Services sector** — up 7.1%, led by financial services, IT-BPO, and wholesale trade
- **Public construction** — up 12.4%, driven by infrastructure spending under the national budget
- **Consumer spending** — up 5.8%, supported by remittances and improved employment numbers

National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA) Secretary Arsenio Balisacan attributed the strong performance to the government's continued infrastructure push and the resilience of the services sector.

The Philippines now ranks among the fastest-growing economies in Southeast Asia for the quarter, alongside Vietnam and Indonesia. The government maintained its full-year GDP growth target of 6.0% to 7.0% for 2026.
