About Kevin Armstrong

IT shouldn't cost more every year. Kevin Armstrong is Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, where he leads a ten-person identity practice across the United States, Argentina, and India. With 20+ years in enterprise security, his focus is simple: reduce execution risk, improve margin discipline, and make identity measurable.

His work spans Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, with large-scale delivery including a 10,000+-user multi-domain consolidation and standing-privilege reduction across complex hybrid environments. His current focus is non-human identity governance: the service principals, app registrations, managed identities, and credentials that hold real permissions and usually have no named owner. On one engagement, that meant enumerating and remediating roughly 1,700 service principals across acquired estates. He leads with an engineering-first approach and strong business alignment.

Where I've Been

As Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, I lead a ten-person identity practice across three countries and direct multi-platform modernization across Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, unifying workforce and customer identity strategy while reducing long-term platform risk and ownership cost. That work includes SOX-scoped IT general controls for public-company clients, where the evidence has to survive an auditor's sample.

At Liberty Latin America, I executed a high-risk M&A infrastructure consolidation, merging multiple Active Directory domains and deploying Zero Trust controls that materially reduced privileged access exposure across a large, distributed enterprise spanning multiple countries.

The thread through all of it: identity is the control plane. Get it right, and security, compliance, and cost follow. Get it wrong, and everything downstream becomes expensive.
