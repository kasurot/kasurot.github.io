About Kevin Armstrong

Kevin Armstrong is a Director of Security / Identity leader with 20+ years of experience building and operating enterprise identity, security, and governance programs. His work spans Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, with large-scale delivery including a 10,000+ user multi-domain consolidation and standing-privilege reduction across complex hybrid environments.

Kevin leads with an engineering-first approach and strong business alignment. His focus is reducing execution risk, improving margin discipline, and turning identity programs into controllable, measurable business assets rather than recurring cost centers.

What I Do

For Enterprise Security & Identity Programs

  • Identity Program Leadership: Entra ID, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint strategy, governance, and operational ownership.
  • Zero Trust & Privileged Access: Conditional access, PIM/JIT, and standing privilege reduction across workforce and customer environments.
  • Compliance Evidence Delivery: Access certification, SoD, entitlement governance, and audit-ready control evidence for ISO 27001, SOC 2, and NIST-aligned programs.

For PE, Portfolio Companies & Executive Engagements

  • Post-Acquisition Identity Risk Review: Rapid assessment of identity sprawl, privileged access exposure, and integration risk across merged environments.
  • Fractional CISO / Director Advisory: Board-ready governance, security strategy, and operating-model design without full-time overhead.

Professional Background

Most recently, Kevin served as Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, where he led multi-platform identity modernization across Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, unifying workforce and customer identity strategy while reducing long-term platform risk and ownership cost.

Prior to GCA, Kevin executed a high-risk M&A infrastructure consolidation at Liberty Latin America, merging multiple Active Directory domains and deploying Zero Trust controls that materially reduced privileged access exposure across a large, distributed enterprise.
