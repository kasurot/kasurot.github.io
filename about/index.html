I'm Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, where I lead the Microsoft identity, Okta and PAM practices: ten engineers and specialists across the United States, Argentina and India. I built all three from zero.

Most identity programs can name every person with access. Almost none can name the service principals, app registrations and managed identities holding the same rights. That gap is most of what I work on: inventorying roughly 1,700 service principals across acquired estates, putting a name against each one, and sequencing the remediation by annual loss expectancy rather than by whoever complains loudest.

On one engagement I'm the named IAM control owner in the client's GRC tooling, presenting the evidence in audit walkthroughs and regulatory exam interviews. Attestation stays with the client's management; the evidence is mine. That line is the job.

Where I've Been

GCA Technology Services, 2021 to present. I run identity operations for enterprise clients: Entra ID and Okta as managed services, risk-tiered access recertification across 5,000+ identities, and phishing-resistant rollouts that replaced phishable MFA with passkeys and security keys across multiple customer workforces. SailPoint delivery I coordinate with the firm's SailPoint practice rather than own.

Liberty Latin America, 2017 to 2021, including Cable & Wireless before the acquisition. I consolidated five Active Directory domains and 10,000+ users into a single tenant, cut standing privileged accounts by 40%, and operated the identity controls tested under SOX at a publicly traded company: I wrote the tests, supplied the evidence, and sat with the auditors. My name was not on the signing line.

Earlier, 2003 to 2015: systems and infrastructure engineering. At TaxSlayer I moved services onto Group Managed Service Accounts, removed static service account credentials, and operated the identity controls tested under PCI DSS and SOX. That is the same non-human identity problem I now govern at enterprise scale.

Engagements have spanned insurance, healthcare, retail and telecom. The thread through all of it: identity is the control plane. Get it right and security, compliance and cost follow. Get it wrong and everything downstream becomes expensive.
