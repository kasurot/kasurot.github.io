About Kevin Armstrong

Professional Background

Most recently, Kevin served as Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, where he engineered a strategic pivot from project-based consulting (NRE) to high-margin Managed Identity subscriptions (ARR), creating a scalable recurring revenue stream within the services portfolio.

He directed a holistic identity modernization portfolio leveraging Auth0, Okta, Ping Identity, and Microsoft Entra—unifying Customer and Workforce identity strategies while designing vendor-neutral CIAM patterns that prevented lock-in and reduced long-term TCO.

Prior to GCA, Kevin executed a high-risk M&A infrastructure consolidation at Liberty Latin America, merging 5 Active Directory domains (10,000+ users) and deploying Zero Trust architecture that reduced standing privileged access by 40%.
