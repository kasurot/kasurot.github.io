About Kevin Armstrong

Kevin Armstrong is a Director of Security / Identity leader with 20+ years of experience building and operating enterprise identity, security, and governance programs. He specializes in identity architecture, privileged access governance, and operational risk reduction across complex multi-platform environments.

Kevin leads with an engineering-first approach and strong business alignment. His focus is reducing execution risk, improving margin discipline, and turning identity programs into controllable, measurable business assets rather than recurring cost centers.

What I Do

For Enterprise Security & Identity Programs

  • Identity Program Leadership: Entra ID, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint program strategy, implementation governance, and operational ownership.
  • Zero Trust & Privileged Access: Zero Trust architecture, conditional access, PIM/JIT, and standing privilege reduction across workforce and customer environments.
  • Compliance Evidence Delivery: IGA-aligned access certification, SoD, entitlement governance, and audit-ready control evidence for ISO 27001, SOC 2, and NIST programs.

For PE, Portfolio Companies & Executive Engagements

  • Post-Acquisition Identity Risk Review: Rapid assessment of identity sprawl, privileged access exposure, and integration risk across merged environments.
  • Fractional CISO / Director Advisory: Board-ready governance, security strategy, and operating-model design without full-time overhead.
  • Margin-Focused Transformation: Identity-driven cost reduction, vendor rationalization, and operational modernization aligned to business execution.

Professional Background

Most recently, Kevin served as Director of Professional Services at GCA Technology Services, where he led identity modernization across Microsoft Entra, Okta, Ping Identity, and SailPoint, unifying workforce and customer identity strategy while reducing long-term platform risk and ownership cost.

He directed a strategic shift from project-based delivery to managed identity services, building recurring-revenue capability while improving operational consistency and control maturity.

Prior to GCA, Kevin executed a high-risk M&A infrastructure consolidation at Liberty Latin America, merging multiple Active Directory domains and deploying Zero Trust controls that materially reduced privileged access exposure across a large, distributed enterprise.
